4 Key Properties

Solid
  • Close together
  • Keeps its shape
  • Vibrates in place
Liquid
  • Close together
  • Slides past each other
  • Takes the shape of its container
Gas
  • Fills the whole jar
  • Far apart
  • Bounces everywhere

5 Why It Changes

Heating the particles will give them energy and make them move faster and spread apart. Cooling takes energy away, so they slow down and move closer together.

6 Limitation

Real particles are far too small to see, even with a microscope, and there are more than trillions of them.
